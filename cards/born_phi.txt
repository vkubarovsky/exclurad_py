! ------------------------------------------------------------------
!  phi -> K+ K- electroproduction, CLAS12 RG-A Fall 2018, BORN ONLY
!
!  This card and its twin (rad_phi.txt) differ in TWO fields, iborn and itarg,
!  and in nothing else.  The pair forms N_rad/N_born, so any third
!  difference would put something into that ratio that is not radiation.
!
!  WHY TWO AND NOT ONE.  Until 2026-10-21 the pair differed only in iborn,
!  and that symmetry was the mistake: whatever is present in BOTH arms
!  cancels in the ratio and is therefore NOT corrected for.  The published
!  number is sigma_Born at a definite beam energy, so the pre-vertex
!  external radiator belongs in the radiative arm and must be absent from
!  the Born one -- itarg 1 here against itarg 0 there.  Left on in both,
!  the ratio returns a Born cross section smeared over the beam-energy
!  distribution in the target, which nothing can be compared against.
!
!  Box decided by VPK 2026-10-15:
!     t          -6.00 .. -0.02   (was -3.00 rad / -8.0 born)
!     vmin        0.0    (soft/hard split chosen per event)
!     vz         -5.5 -0.5   as for J/psi: the RG-A F18 nominal cell
!
!  WHY THE t FLOOR IS SO LOW.  t_min(W2,Q2) runs from 0.005 near W2 = 20.8,
!  Q2 = 0.4 to 3.9 at threshold -- a factor 800 -- and the generator enforces
!  it per event (checked: 0 of 20000 events below their own t_min, smallest
!  t' = 8e-5).  A floor on ABSOLUTE |t| is therefore an inhomogeneous cut on
!  t': at -0.20 it removed t' up to 0.195 in the high-W2 / low-Q2 corner and
!  nothing at all near threshold, and that is a cut one would afterwards fit
!  as a diffractive slope.  At -0.02 the residual is at most 0.015 anywhere.
!  Measured on four 20k Born runs over this box:
!
!     floor   sigma     largest t' cut   protons below 321 MeV/c
!     -0.20   81.3 %        0.195                 0 %
!     -0.10   91.4 %        0.095                ~0 %
!     -0.05   97.4 %        0.045               6.2 %
!     -0.02  100.0 %        0.015               8.6 %
!
!  Price of -0.02: 8.6 % of events carry a proton nothing reconstructs.
!  The ceiling never binds -- the largest |t| generated is 5.93.
!
!  Q2 0.40-10.0 means the SCATTERED ELECTRON IS IN THE FORWARD DETECTOR,
!  not the Forward Tagger -- this is electroproduction, a different
!  topology from the J/psi photoproduction campaign that used the same
!  four GEMC configurations.
!
!  vcut = 0: no cut on the missing mass at generation.  The resolution
!  tail has to flow in from above the cut, so the cut is applied offline
!  on the reconstructed MX^2(e'pK+K-), never here.
! ------------------------------------------------------------------
phi.diffrad_vm_pdet                ! model  <channel>.<vintage>   (--models to list)
10.6                               ! ebeam (GeV)
100000                             ! nev
1                                  ! iborn   1 = Born only, 0 = full RC
4.00 20.80                         ! W2 range (GeV^2)
0.40 10.0                          ! Q2 range (GeV^2)
-6.00 -0.02                        ! t = (p-p')^2 from the MEASURED proton (t_kind=target); see the header
0.0                                ! vcut   MX^2(e'p) - m_V^2 (GeV^2); 0 = no cut, cut offline
-0.30                              ! t_nucl  (GeV^2) for pi0/eta models; ignored for vector mesons
0                                  ! ihel    +1, -1, or 0 = helicity-averaged
20261015                           ! seed
1                                  ! idecay  1 = decay vector meson to K+K- isotropically
./exclurad_out                     ! output root -- RELATIVE on purpose.
0.25 0.90                          ! y range  (nu/E)
0.001 0.60                         ! xB range
none                               ! iacc    acceptance preset: none | jpsi
0.0                                ! vmin    soft/hard split (GeV^2); 0 = choose per event
0                                  ! itarg   0 = NO pre-vertex external radiator.
!   THE BORN ARM MUST NOT RADIATE, and this field is the whole difference between
!   this card and the one that produced born_itarg1/.  The rule is that whatever
!   is present in BOTH arms cancels in N_rad/N_born and is therefore NOT corrected
!   for; only what differs between them is removed.  The published number is
!   sigma_Born at a definite beam energy, so the external radiator belongs in the
!   radiative arm and must be absent here.  Left on in both arms -- as it was until
!   2026-10-21 -- the ratio returns a Born cross section smeared over the
!   beam-energy distribution in the target, which nothing can be compared against.
!   Size of the effect, 5 cm LH2, mean bt 0.00374: 2.54 % of trials lose more than
!   0.1 % of the beam energy, 1.70 % lose more than 1 %, mean loss 0.34 %.
!   NOTE _draw_vertex runs regardless of born_only, so itarg=1 radiates even when
!   iborn=1.  That is why this had to be changed rather than being harmless.
-5.5 -0.5                          ! vertex z range, cm -- the RG-A F18 nominal cell (as J/psi)
0.0                                ! ptarg   unpolarised target
none                               ! fermi   free proton
0.0 180.0                          ! theta_e  no window: the Q2/y/W2 box already bounds it; select offline
