# Porting independent_tail.py to the pi0/eta production bin

Status 2026-10-08: **port built and self-validated; first physics comparison
blocked by a finding about the model, not about the tool.**

## What the port adds

`Point(..., mode="target", model_name="pi0.amp2614")`.

1. **Five structure functions.** The J/psi tool built `H^{mu nu}` inline from
   `sigma_T` and `sigma_L` only. pi0/eta need `sigma_TT`, `sigma_LT`,
   `sigma_LTp`, which are AZIMUTHAL structures and so need the detected
   hadron's momentum to fix the X, Y basis. `_LH` now calls exclurad_py's own
   `hadronic_tensor`, i.e. the tensor CONVENTION is shared -- exactly as the
   T/L normalisation already was for J/psi -- while the leptonic side (Dirac
   spinors, full electron mass) and the phase space stay independent.

2. **The target convention.** `t_kind="target"` holds `t_N = (p-p')^2` fixed,
   so unlike `cm_angle` the detected proton's DIRECTION is not frozen: it
   follows v through `E_det(v)`, by the same formula as `kinematics.py`
   `T_TARGET`. At fixed t the Jacobian factor is 1 (`d3p/2E = (p/2) dE dcos
   dphi` with `dcos = dt/(2|q|p)`, so p cancels), as in `meson_t`. `kf` is
   frozen at the observed point, as `ProtonDetectedModel` does in generation.

## THE FINDING: the photon-angle integral cannot converge past ~1e-3

The J/psi tool converges its angle integral to 1e-6. In the pi0/eta target bin
it does not, and more nodes do not help:

    v = 1.5, nt = 240, nps = 24 .. 768:   0.25456 0.25465 0.25454 0.25486 0.25455 0.25457
    v = 1.5, nps = 384, nt = 80 .. 360:   0.25431 0.25520 0.25455 0.25473

Scatter at ~1e-3 with no trend in either grid. That is a DISCONTINUITY in the
integrand, not an under-resolved smooth function, and the cause is the model:

    pi0.amp2614 validity:  W2 (2.2, 16.0)  Q2 (1.15, 8.31)  |t| (0.0, 1.8)

    v       W2~ reached      Q2~ reached     fraction with sigma_T == 0
    0.05    3.24 -  8.94     0.801 - 2.058        0.0 %
    0.50    1.26 -  8.44     0.297 - 2.566        0.2 %
    1.50    1.19 -  7.29     0.189 - 3.634        1.0 %
    2.20    1.30 -  6.45     0.154 - 4.361        0.9 %

At vcut = 0 the radiative integral reaches `W2~ = 1.19` and `Q2~ = 0.19`,
far below the model's own floors of 2.2 and 1.15, and on about 1 % of the
photon-angle domain the model returns EXACTLY ZERO -- a hard step inside the
integration region. Gauss-Legendre across a step gives O(1/N) error with
scatter, which is what is measured.

This is the same phenomenon as `sf_usage.out_of_range_frac = 0.55-0.59` in the
campaign sidecars, seen from the integrand side.

**Consequence for the study.** Comparing the independent tail against
`rc_factor` to better than ~1e-3 is not meaningful until the edge is handled,
because BOTH codes are integrating across the same step with different grids
and will differ by wherever their nodes fall. The question "is the 20-30 %
hard tail correct" is well posed only once the model's behaviour at the edge
is a decision rather than an accident.
