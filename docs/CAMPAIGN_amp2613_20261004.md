Requested by VPK: all four channels, rad and born, unpolarised target,
100 000 events each.  **Delivered complete: 8/8 samples, 800 000 events, no
FATAL, no stage-2 breach.**

    /volatile/clas12/vpk/night_amp2613_20261004/<channel>/<mode>/lund_osg/

## Normalisation and the box-averaged radiative factor

sigma_nb is a per-chunk Monte Carlo estimate, so it scatters; the spread is
statistical (1.0-1.8 % rms over 20 chunks of 5000, as expected).

| channel | rad | born | rad/born |
|---|---|---|---|
| pi0p | 1.45123 +- 0.0205 | 0.90936 +- 0.0125 | 1.596 |
| etap | 0.44482 +- 0.0046 | 0.39347 +- 0.0038 | 1.130 |
| **pi0n** | **1.02578 +- 0.0155** | **0.37653 +- 0.0052** | **2.724** |
| etan | 0.21749 +- 0.0040 | 0.17696 +- 0.0018 | 1.229 |

**rad/born is NOT a radiative correction factor.**  The cards run `vcut = 0`,
so the rad samples carry the ENTIRE radiative tail with no missing-mass cut --
that cut is an analysis cut and is applied offline.  The ratio is the
box-integrated sigma_obs/sigma_born over the full tail.

pi0n's 2.72, against 1.60 for pi0p, is the number to look at.  It is consistent
with everything else that channel does: the highest stage-2 bound (8.34), the
longest jobs (1126 ms/event), and the largest hard-photon fraction (79.7 %
against 67.4 % for pi0p).  It is also the channel where amp2613 is least
constrained -- twelve data points in one kinematic bin against four hundred for
the proton.  Treat the neutron samples as indicative.

## LUND structure, verified

Soft/hard split at `vmin = 1e-4`, first chunk of each rad sample:

| channel | 5 particles (soft) | 6 particles (hard photon) | hard fraction |
|---|---|---|---|
| pi0p | 1628 | 3372 | 67.4 % |
| etap | 2297 | 2703 | 54.1 % |
| pi0n | 1016 | 3984 | 79.7 % |
| etan | 2135 | 2865 | 57.3 % |

Born samples are 5 particles throughout, as they must be.  A hard event reads
e' / recoil / meson / **radiated gamma (parent 0)** / two decay gammas (parent
3).  Target PID is 2212 for pi0p and etap, **2112** for pi0n and etan.
Helicity is in LUND column 5 and takes +-1 per event, which is what `ihel = 0`
means: helicity-AVERAGED cross section, label drawn per event.
